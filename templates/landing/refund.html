Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: January 2024

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all subscription plans. If you're not satisfied, we'll refund your payment - no questions asked.

1. Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • You request a refund within 7 days of your purchase
  • This is your first refund request for this subscription type
  • The subscription was purchased directly through BlinkStudy (not through third-party resellers)

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please:

  • Email us at support@blinkstudy.in with subject "Refund Request"
  • Include your registered email/phone number
  • Mention your order ID or transaction ID
  • Briefly explain the reason for your refund (optional but helpful)

3. Refund Processing Time

  • Review: We will review your request within 24-48 hours
  • Processing: Approved refunds are processed within 3-5 business days
  • Credit: Depending on your payment method, it may take an additional 5-10 business days to reflect in your account

4. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time:

  • Go to Settings > Subscription in the app
  • Click "Cancel Subscription"
  • Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period
  • No refund is provided for the remaining period after the 7-day window

Note: Cancelling your subscription will NOT automatically delete your account or data. Your data will be retained as per our Privacy Policy.

5. Non-Refundable Items

The following are NOT eligible for refunds:

  • Requests made after 7 days of purchase
  • Repeat refund requests for the same subscription type
  • Promotional or discounted subscriptions (unless stated otherwise)
  • Subscriptions purchased through Google Play or App Store (please contact them directly)

6. Partial Refunds

In some cases, we may offer partial refunds for:

  • Extended service outages affecting your usage
  • Technical issues that couldn't be resolved
  • Billing errors on our part

7. Contact for Billing Issues

For any billing-related queries, please contact:

  • Email: support@blinkstudy.in
  • Response time: Within 24 hours

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting. Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
