Our Story

BlinkStudy was born from a simple observation: millions of Indian students struggle with their studies, not because they lack intelligence, but because they lack access to quality guidance when they need it most - at 2 AM before an exam, during a confusing homework problem, or when preparing for competitive exams.

We built BlinkStudy to be that reliable study companion that's always available. Using cutting-edge AI technology, we've created tools that understand how Indian students learn and what they need - from CBSE and ICSE board exams to JEE and NEET preparation.

Today, thousands of students across India use BlinkStudy daily to scan problems, generate quizzes, watch whiteboard explanations, and chat with their AI tutor. And we're just getting started.
