Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your registered mobile number or email, and we'll send you an OTP to reset your password.
How do I upgrade my plan?
Open the app, go to Settings > Subscription, and choose the plan you want. Payment can be made via UPI, cards, or net banking.
Can I get a refund?
Yes, we offer refunds within 7 days of purchase if you're not satisfied. Please contact support@blinkstudy.in with your order details.
Why is Scan & Solve not working?
Make sure you have a stable internet connection and camera permissions enabled. Try taking a clearer photo with good lighting. If the problem persists, contact support.
How do I delete my account?
To delete your account, you need to fill out an account deletion request form. Click here to fill the form. Our team will process your request within 48 hours. Note that this action is irreversible and all your data will be permanently deleted.
