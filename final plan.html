BlinkStudy AI targets ₹10 crore gross revenue in 2026 through a 3-stream model: Subscriptions (60%), Advertising (25%), and Referrals & In-App Purchases (15%). With optimized pricing, lean cost operations, and aggressive user acquisition, the projected net profit is ₹5.35 crore (53.5% margin).
